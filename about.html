About Institute

Institute for Drug Delivery and Biomedical Research

The Institute for Drug Delivery and Biomedical Research (IDBR) is a research-driven organization dedicated to advancing drug delivery science, topical product development, skin permeation studies, pharmaceutical innovation, and translational biomedical research.

Through scientific excellence, practical training, and collaborative partnerships, IDBR serves as a bridge between academia and industry. Our work focuses on transforming scientific discoveries into meaningful healthcare solutions while supporting the development of future researchers and healthcare innovators.

Our Mission

Driving Scientific Excellence

  • Train future scientists and research professionals
  • Support pharmaceutical innovation
  • Develop advanced drug delivery systems
  • Advance topical and transdermal research
  • Bridge academia and industry
  • Promote translational biomedical research
Our Vision

Global Research Leadership

To become a globally recognized center of excellence in drug delivery, skin permeation science, topical product development, and translational biomedical research through innovation, collaboration, and scientific excellence.
